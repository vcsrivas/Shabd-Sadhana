About meanings

Meanings, usage examples and synonyms come from two dictionaries:

Open English WordNet, built on Princeton WordNet 3.0. Licence: CC BY 4.0 and the WordNet licence.

Webster's Revised Unabridged Dictionary (1913), from Project Gutenberg. Public domain. Webster's entries are over a century old, so some spellings and examples are old-fashioned.

The Cambridge and Oxford buttons on every word open its entry on those dictionaries' websites.
